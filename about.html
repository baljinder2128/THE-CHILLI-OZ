Our story

A cosy neighbourhood café built around one idea: bold spice and comforting sweetness belong together.

Who we are

Flavour, not just heat

The Chilli'Oz serves specialty coffee, freshly baked pastries and gourmet toasties, alongside a menu of sweet and savoury signatures infused with subtle spice. Think chilli-chocolate brownies, cardamom buns, a spiced chai latte and a chicken & chilli mayo toastie.

Every signature item is made to balance a little warmth with something comforting, so it's interesting without being fiery.

We're open from 5am on weekdays for the early crowd, with a relaxed brunch on Saturdays, and room for about 30 people inside and out.
